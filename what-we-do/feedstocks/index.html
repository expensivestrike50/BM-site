Feedstocks Material Intelligence FAQs

Clear answers to the top questions customers ask before getting started.

What kinds of waste streams can you assess?

We assess concrete, masonry, and industrial byproduct streams, using their characteristics and the performance your product requires.

Do I need to share my full production data?

No. We start with the feedstock details, equipment and goals you are comfortable sharing, and expand the analysis as the work progresses.

How do you account for material variability?

We track how properties change across batches and sources, and build those ranges into every recommendation we make.

Will I need to change my current equipment?

Usually not. Recommendations are designed around the equipment you already run, so the path to validation stays practical.

How long does a feedstock review take?

A first review can be shared within a few weeks, depending on how much data is available and how many streams are involved.
