Did you know?

Construction and industrial activity generates around 3.5 billion tons of waste every year, while the world spends $1.48 trillion on construction materials. Much of that waste could become the feedstock for better materials.

About Better Materials

Our Mission

We built Better Materials so manufacturers do not have to choose between performance, cost and carbon. Our goal is to help turn waste streams into high-performance construction materials, using AI to reduce expensive trial and error and physical testing to prove what works.

How? By making waste-derived materials practical for real manufacturing.

Our Approach

Waste as Feedstock

Why it matters

Ash, slag and demolition rubble hold real value. Reusing them cuts landfill waste and lowers the carbon of binders and aggregates.

AI With Physical Proof

How we develop

Machine learning explores far more formulations than a lab could test, and targeted experiments confirm what works before production.
